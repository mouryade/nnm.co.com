Nurturing Indian Performing Arts Since 1990

Established in 1990 under the visionary guidance of Smt. Sankari De, Nataraj Nritya Mandir (HOW/62) is a premier dance institution in Sankrail, Howrah. We combine ancient aesthetic traditions with holistic physical discipline, preparing students for board diploma examinations and prestigious national stage recitals.
